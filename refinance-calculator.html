Refinance Break-Even Calculator

Compare your current payment with a new loan payment and estimate how long it takes for monthly savings to cover closing costs.

Who this calculator is for

Homeowners or borrowers considering a rate-and-term refinance who want a quick sense of monthly savings and break-even time. It is a simplified model—not a lender disclosure.

How break-even is estimated

We compute a fixed payment on the current balance for remaining term at the current rate, and a new payment at the new rate and term. Monthly savings ≈ current payment − new payment. Break-even months ≈ closing costs ÷ monthly savings (when savings are positive).

Worked examples

Example. $300,000 balance, 7% with 25 years left vs 6% for 30 years with $4,000 costs. Payment usually drops; break-even depends on how large the savings are each month.

Frequently asked questions

Should I reset to 30 years?

A longer new term can lower the payment but may increase total interest. Compare total cost, not only the monthly bill.

Are points included?

Include points and lender fees in closing costs for a fairer break-even.

What if the new payment is higher?

The tool will show that. Refinancing may still be considered for other reasons (cash-out, shorter term), which this simple model does not fully capture.
