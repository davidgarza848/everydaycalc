How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the loan (principal) amount — the amount you borrow after any down payment.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (APR) as a percentage.
  3. Choose the loan term in years (commonly 15 or 30).
  4. Review the estimated monthly payment, total paid, and total interest.

When to use it

Estimate the fixed monthly principal-and-interest payment on a home loan. Enter the loan amount, interest rate, and term to see payment, total interest, and total paid.

Formula

Payment = P × r(1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

Example

A $350,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years has a monthly principal-and-interest payment of about $2,213. Over the full term you would pay roughly $447,000 in total interest (taxes and insurance not included).

Frequently asked questions

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. This estimates principal and interest only. Your full housing payment may also include property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and PMI.

Is this the same as a mortgage quote?

No. Lenders use their own rates, fees, and underwriting. Use this for a planning estimate only.

What about adjustable-rate mortgages?

This tool assumes a fixed rate for the full term. ARMs change over time and are not modeled here.
