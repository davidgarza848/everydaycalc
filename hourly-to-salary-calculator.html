Hourly to Salary Calculator

Turn an hourly rate into weekly, monthly, and annual gross pay. Useful when comparing job offers or budgeting from an hourly wage.

How to use

  1. Enter your hourly wage.
  2. Enter hours worked per week.
  3. Enter paid weeks per year (often 52).
  4. See weekly, monthly average, and annual gross pay.

Formula

Annual = hourly × hours/week × weeks/year

Example

At $22/hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $45,760 gross per year. Weekly gross is $880.

FAQ

Is this before or after taxes?

Before taxes (gross). Use the paycheck calculator for a rough net estimate.

What if I work overtime?

This tool uses a fixed weekly hours figure. Use the overtime or time card tools for OT.
