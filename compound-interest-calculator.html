Compound Interest Calculator

See how an investment or savings balance can grow when interest compounds over time. Choose monthly, quarterly, annual, or daily compounding.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your starting amount (principal).
  2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  3. Enter the number of years the money will grow.
  4. Choose how often interest compounds (monthly is common for savings).

Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)^(n·t)

Example

Starting with $10,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about $16,470 — roughly $6,470 in interest earned.

Frequently asked questions

What is compounding?

Compounding means interest is added to the balance, so future interest is earned on both the original amount and prior interest.

Does this include regular deposits?

Not in this version. It models a single starting amount left to grow. Recurring deposits would increase the final balance further.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Rates change, and fees or taxes can reduce real returns. This is an educational estimate.
