OUR APPROACH

Without the fuss.

EverydayCalc is designed to make common calculations easier. Use the tools for quick estimates, understand the assumptions behind the numbers, and make decisions with more confidence. Results are estimates and should be checked when accuracy is especially important.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. You can use the calculators without creating an account.

Are the results official financial or tax advice?

No. Results are estimates. Paycheck estimates use the tax rate you enter and do not calculate actual federal, state, or local tax liability. Loan results assume a fixed rate and regular monthly payments.

How does EverydayCalc make money?

The site may earn revenue from advertising after it is connected to an advertising provider and approved. No ads are active in this starter version.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
