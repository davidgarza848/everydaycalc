QUICK GUIDES

How these calculators work

Short explanations so you understand the numbers — not just the result.

Paycheck estimates

Enter your gross pay and an estimated tax/deduction rate. Net pay is gross × (1 − rate). This is a rough guide only — real withholding depends on filing status, state tax, benefits, and more.

Loan payments

We use the standard fixed-rate formula: payment = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. Fees and variable rates are not included.

Overtime & schedules

Regular hours are paid at your base rate; overtime uses the multiplier you enter (often 1.5). The schedule tool subtracts unpaid breaks and supports overnight shifts. Always confirm with your employer’s rules.

BMI

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Categories follow common WHO adult ranges. BMI is a screening number only — it does not measure body fat or health directly.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. You can use the calculators without creating an account.

Are the results official financial or tax advice?

No. Results are estimates. Paycheck estimates use the tax rate you enter and do not calculate actual federal, state, or local tax liability. Loan results assume a fixed rate and regular monthly payments.

How does EverydayCalc make money?

The site uses Google AdSense advertising to help cover costs and keep the tools free. No account is required to use the calculators.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens. You can also bookmark individual calculators using the address bar after opening them.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Calculations run entirely in your browser. The values you type are not sent to EverydayCalc servers.
