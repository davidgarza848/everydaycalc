Overtime Pay Calculator

Estimate pay for overtime hours using your base rate and a multiplier such as 1.5 for time-and-a-half.

Formula

OT pay = OT hours × rate × multiplier

Example

6 OT hours at $18/hr with 1.5× = $162.
