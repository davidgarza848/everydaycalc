Car Loan Calculator

Estimate a fixed monthly payment for an auto loan. Enter the amount financed, APR, and term in months to see payment and total interest.

Who this calculator is for

Shoppers comparing dealer or bank offers, or checking whether a vehicle payment fits a budget. Term is entered in months (for example 36, 48, 60, or 72).

How the calculation works

Payment = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Same fixed-installment formula as other loans. Taxes, title, warranties, and gap insurance are not included unless you add them into the loan amount.

Worked examples

Example 1. $28,000 at 6% for 60 months → about $541/mo.

Example 2. $22,000 at 7.5% for 48 months → higher payment, less total interest than stretching to 72 months.

Frequently asked questions

Should I finance taxes and fees?

If they are rolled into the loan, include them in the loan amount for a more realistic payment.

Is a longer term better?

It lowers the monthly payment but usually increases total interest and can leave you underwater longer.

Does rate depend on new vs used?

Often yes. Enter the APR the lender quotes for your credit and vehicle type.
