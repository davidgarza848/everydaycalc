Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

EverydayCalc provides free browser-based calculators at evrydaycalc.com. This policy describes how the site works and what data third-party services may process.

Information you enter into calculators

The calculator tools process the numbers and dates you enter in your browser to produce results. The calculator code does not send those entries to an EverydayCalc server. Your inputs stay on your device for the calculation.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements and help keep the tools free. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance.

You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage ad personalization at Google’s Advertising page and the Ads Settings page. You may also visit the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page or similar industry tools.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies or local storage may be used by advertising partners (such as Google) for the purposes described above. EverydayCalc itself does not set account or tracking cookies for the calculator features.

Analytics

No separate analytics service is currently configured beyond what advertising partners may collect in connection with ad delivery and measurement. If dedicated analytics are added later, this policy will be updated.

Children

This site is intended as a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date above will be revised when substantive changes are made.

Contact

For questions or corrections, use the contact page.
