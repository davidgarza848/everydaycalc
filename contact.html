Contact EverydayCalc

We welcome feedback about calculator accuracy, unclear results, accessibility problems, privacy questions, and ideas for new tools. EverydayCalc is a small project; we read messages and use them to improve the site.

How to reach us

Email: hello@evrydaycalc.com

For public bug reports or feature ideas you may also open an issue on the project repository:

Open a GitHub issue

What to include

  • Which calculator you used
  • What you entered and what result you expected
  • Your device and browser if something looks broken

Please do not send passwords, bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal data. Calculator inputs are meant to stay on your device; we do not need full financial account details to help with a formula question.

Response time

This is not a 24/7 support desk. We aim to review messages when we can. Urgent financial, tax, medical, or legal decisions should be handled with a qualified professional—not delayed for a website reply.
