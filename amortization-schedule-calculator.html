Amortization Schedule Calculator

Estimate a fixed monthly payment and see total interest plus how much of your first year goes to interest versus principal. Designed for fixed-rate home or personal loans.

Who this calculator is for

Use this when you want more than a single payment number—especially how costly the early years are in interest, or how term length changes lifetime cost. It pairs with the mortgage and loan payment tools.

How amortization works

Each payment is the same dollar amount. Interest for the month is charged on the remaining balance; the rest of the payment reduces principal. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal.

Payment = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Frequently asked questions

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. Principal and interest only.

Can I model extra payments?

Not in this version. Extra principal payments reduce interest and shorten the loan.

Is this a full month-by-month table?

This tool summarizes payment, totals, and year-one split. Use it for planning estimates.

Fixed rate only?

Yes. ARMs are not modeled.
