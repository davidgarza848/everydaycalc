BMI Calculator

Calculate body mass index from height and weight in metric or imperial units. See the common WHO adult category. BMI is a screening number only.

Advertisement

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter weight and choose kg or lb.
  2. Enter height and choose cm or in.
  3. See BMI and category.

Formula

BMI = weight(kg) / height(m)²

Example

70 kg and 175 cm → height 1.75 m → BMI ≈ 22.9 (normal weight range for many adult charts).

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. It is a screening tool and does not measure body fat or health directly.

Do the categories apply to everyone?

Common adult ranges may not fit children, athletes, or some medical contexts.

Metric or imperial?

Both work; the tool converts as needed.
