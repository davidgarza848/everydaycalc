Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature, and speed between common metric and US units. Results update as you type—no account needed.

What you can convert

Length: mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards, miles

Weight: mg, g, kg, ounces, pounds, stone

Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin

Speed: m/s, km/h, mph, knots

How conversions work

Length, weight, and speed use fixed international conversion factors through a base unit (meter, kilogram, or meter per second). Temperature uses exact formulas (for example, °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9). Displayed values are rounded for readability; tiny rounding differences can appear compared with specialized lab tools.

Worked examples

5 ft 10 in → enter 70 inches to centimeters ≈ 177.8 cm

150 lb ≈ 68.04 kg

72 °F ≈ 22.22 °C

60 mph ≈ 96.56 km/h

Tips

For BMI, convert height to inches or centimeters and weight to pounds or kilograms so the units match the BMI calculator. For cooking or shipping, double-check whether you need fluid ounces versus weight ounces—this tool handles mass, not volume liquids.

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

They use standard fixed factors and exact temperature formulas. Displayed results are rounded.

Can I convert height for BMI?

Yes. Convert height, then open the BMI calculator with matching weight units.

Why isn’t currency included?

Currency needs live exchange rates. This converter stays offline-friendly with fixed physical units.

Do you store my numbers?

No. Everything runs in your browser. See the privacy policy.
