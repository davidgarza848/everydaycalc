How to use this converter

  1. Choose a category: length, weight, temperature, or speed.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Pick the From and To units. The result updates instantly.

What you can convert

Length: mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards, miles

Weight: mg, g, kg, ounces, pounds, stone

Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin

Speed: m/s, km/h, mph, knots

Examples

FAQ

Are these conversions exact?

Length, weight, and speed use standard fixed factors. Temperature uses exact formulas. Displayed values are rounded for readability.

Can I convert height for BMI?

Yes. Convert height to cm or inches, then use the BMI calculator with matching weight units.
