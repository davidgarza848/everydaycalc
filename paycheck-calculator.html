Paycheck Calculator

Estimate how much of your paycheck you take home after deductions. Enter your gross pay per paycheck and an approximate tax or deduction rate. The tool annualizes results based on how often you get paid.

Advertisement

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your gross pay for one paycheck.
  2. Enter an estimated combined tax and deduction rate (for example 20–30%).
  3. Choose how often you are paid.
  4. See estimated net pay and annualized totals instantly.

Formula

Net ≈ Gross × (1 − tax rate)

Example

If gross pay is $2,000 and the tax rate is 20%, net pay is about $1,600. Paid biweekly, that is roughly $41,600 net per year.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official tax calculator?

No. It uses the rate you enter and does not apply federal, state, or local tax tables, pre-tax benefits, or withholding rules.

What tax rate should I use?

A rough combined rate of 20–30% is common for estimates, but your real rate depends on filing status, deductions, and location.

Does it include 401(k) or health insurance?

Not automatically. Raise the deduction rate to approximate those amounts.
