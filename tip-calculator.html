Tip & Split Bill Calculator

Figure out a tip and divide the total among a group. Adjust the tip percent and number of people for a quick per-person amount.

Formula

Per person = (bill + tip) / people

Example

A $60 bill at 20% tip with 2 people: tip $12, total $72, $36 each.

FAQ

What tip percent is typical?

In the U.S., 15–20% is common for table service.
