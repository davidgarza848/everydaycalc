Loan Payment Calculator

Estimate a fixed monthly payment for a loan with a constant interest rate. See total paid and total interest over the life of the loan.

How to use

  1. Enter the loan amount (principal).
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (APR).
  3. Enter the term in years.
  4. Review monthly payment, total paid, and total interest.

Formula

Payment = P × r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1)

Example

A $15,000 loan at 7% APR for 5 years has about $297/month estimated payment.

FAQ

Does this include fees?

No. Principal and interest only for a fixed-rate loan.
