Loan Payment Calculator

Estimate a fixed monthly payment for a personal loan, auto loan, or other installment loan. See how rate and term affect total interest and overall cost.

Who this calculator is for

Use this tool to compare loan offers, check whether a payment fits your budget, or see how a shorter term changes total interest. It assumes a fixed interest rate and equal monthly payments that fully repay the loan by the end of the term.

It does not include origination fees, prepayment penalties, or variable rates unless you adjust the inputs yourself to approximate them.

How the calculation works

Payment = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12), and n is the number of months. The same formula is used for many fixed-rate mortgages; only typical amounts and terms differ.

Worked examples

Example 1 — personal loan. $15,000 at 7% for 5 years → about $297/month, roughly $2,820 in interest over the term.

Example 2 — auto-style loan. $28,000 at 6% for 60 months → about $541/month.

Example 3 — shorter term. Same $15,000 at 7% for 3 years raises the monthly payment but cuts total interest versus five years.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include loan fees?

No. Fees can raise the effective cost. Ask the lender for APR that includes fees when comparing offers.

Can I use this for credit cards?

Not well. Credit cards often use different methods and variable rates. This tool is for fixed installment loans.

What if I pay extra each month?

Extra principal payments reduce interest and shorten the loan. This basic calculator shows the scheduled payment only.

Is a lower payment always better?

Not necessarily. Longer terms lower the monthly payment but usually increase total interest.

Are results a loan approval?

No. Approval depends on credit, income, and lender policy.
