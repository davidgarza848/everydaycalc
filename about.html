About EverydayCalc

EverydayCalc is a free collection of simple calculators for common money, work, shopping, school, and daily-life questions. The goal is straightforward: give you a clear number quickly, explain the assumptions behind it, and stay usable on any phone or computer without creating an account.

What we offer

Tools include paycheck estimates, hourly-to-salary conversion, overtime and work schedules, loan and mortgage payments, compound interest, sales tax and discounts, tips, percentages, grades, age, BMI, days between dates, and unit conversion. Each calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are processed on your device by the page’s scripts and are not sent to EverydayCalc servers for storage.

How the numbers work

Results are estimates for general information. For example, the paycheck tool uses the tax or deduction rate you enter; it does not apply official tax tables. Loan and mortgage tools use the standard fixed-rate payment formula and do not include fees, insurance, or variable rates unless noted. Always verify important decisions with a lender, tax professional, employer, or other qualified source.

Privacy and advertising

EverydayCalc does not require an account. We aim to keep the tools free. The site may use Google AdSense to help cover costs once the site is approved to show ads. Details about cookies and third-party services are in the privacy policy.

Who it’s for

Anyone who needs a quick, transparent calculation—comparing a job offer, planning a home payment, splitting a bill, converting units, or checking a grade—without signing up or installing an app.
